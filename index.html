Niche-focused apparel for underserved audiences

Original designs combining professional identities (nurses, teachers, vets) with their personal interests (cats, dogs, hobbies). Each piece serves a micro-audience with humor, warmth, and craft.

About this portfolio

I create designs at the intersection of Identity × Interest — micro-niches that resonate deeply with specific audiences rather than competing for broad terms. Every design starts from research: what professions are underserved? What inside jokes haven't been turned into wearable art yet?

AI tools are a starting point for image generation, but every niche selection, tagline, color curation, trademark check, and product variant decision is human-led. The result: 70 hyper-specific designs across cottagecore, dark academia, Y2K, mental health awareness, and career milestones.
